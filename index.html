How the calculator works

Each team's expected efficiency (points per 100 possessions) is estimated as its offensive rating plus its opponent's defensive rating minus a neutral 100 baseline. Expected points equal expected efficiency multiplied by possessions divided by 100.

For home-court games, the selected home advantage is added to the home margin by allocating half to each team's projected score. Neutral games use no home advantage. The first-half estimate is a configurable percentage of full-game points.

Data coverage: All 365 current Division I men’s programs have published preseason offensive and defensive ratings, retrieved October 30, 2026. Highlights supplies 358 programs, including all 191 original ratings, whose values are unchanged. Seven supplemental programs use Bart Torvik’s 2027 preseason ratings: Lindenwood, Mercyhurst, New Haven, Queens, Southern Indiana, West Florida, and West Georgia.

Rating sources: Both sources publish adjusted points per 100 possessions, but they use different models. Supplemental teams are labeled in the dropdowns and flagged in the calculator. Cross-source matchups and rankings are provisional; no calibration between providers has been performed. No ratings are fabricated to fill gaps.

Limitations: The locally stored 2026–27 directory includes all 365 programs in the NCAA men’s basketball membership list, verified October 30, 2026. Directory membership does not imply that a team has ratings. No verified team-specific pace, strength-of-schedule recalibration, player availability, recent form, live sportsbook feed or daily auto-updates. The ratings are embedded in this static build and do not update automatically.

Free access: No account or payment required. This prototype runs calculations locally in your browser.
